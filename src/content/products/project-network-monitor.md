  A small device that sits on your desk and shows you whether your
  internet is working and how fast it is. Three LEDs show status —

  results. The 3D-printed case fits neatly next to your router or monitor.

  - ESP32 dev board — programmed and ready
  - 3x colour LEDs — green, amber, red
  - 0.96" OLED display — latency and uptime
  - 3D-printed case — desk-friendly design
  - All wires and a USB-C cable
  - Complete build guide with wiring diagrams and code

  Add more servers to the ping list, adjust the check interval, or wire
  up an extra display for historical graphs. Great for troubleshooting
  and proving to your ISP that it's actually down.
