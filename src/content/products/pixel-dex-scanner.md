**Requires:** Starter Pack (ESP32, breadboard, wires, USB-C cable, buttons, buzzer)

## What you'll learn

- **I2C displays** — communicating with OLED screens
- **Display drivers** — drawing pixels, text, and images
- **Sensor reading** — analog + digital sensor input
- **State machines** — managing scan modes, menus, and transitions
- **Pixel art rendering** — drawing Pokémon silhouettes on a tiny screen

## Difficulty

**Intermediate** — I2C wiring requires attention, 1.5 hours to build.
