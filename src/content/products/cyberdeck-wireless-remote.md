## What you'll learn

- **WiFi networking** — connecting ESP32 to your home network
- **HTTP requests** — building a simple web server
- **Relay control** — switching devices on/off safely
- **Home automation** — controlling lights from your phone
- **LED strip driving** — colour patterns across multiple pixels

**Intermediate** — relay wiring needs care, 1.5 hours to build.
