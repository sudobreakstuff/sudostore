**Requires:** Starter Pack (ESP32, breadboard, wires, USB-C cable)

## What you'll learn

- **Audio PWM** — generating sound from a microcontroller
- **Tone generation** — frequencies, notes, musical scales
- **Button input** — reading digital signals, debouncing
- **Melody sequences** — playing songs note by note
- **Custom SFX** — record and playback your own sound effects

## Difficulty

**Beginner** — simple wiring, fun result, 45 minutes to build.

## After this kit

Combine with the Cyberdeck Wireless Remote to build a Smart Pikachu — voice-activated LED alerts with Pikachu sounds.
