**Requires:** Starter Pack (ESP32, breadboard, wires, USB-C cable)

## What you'll learn

- **LED colour control** — RGB values, colour wheels, brightness
- **Pattern animations** — chase, pulse, rainbow, breathing effects
- **WiFi notifications** — light up when you get a WhatsApp, email, or alert
- **Arduino libraries** — installing and using FastLED
- **PWM brightness** — smooth dimming without flicker

## Difficulty

**Beginner** — reuses your Starter Pack skills, 1 hour to build.
